# Scenario: finance, payroll and customer-service administrator

Generate a realistic fictional company and a set of everyday problems for the
following job. Use the [shared sandbox authoring instructions](AUTHORING_AGENT.md)
to turn the scenario into a company pack. Keep the result grounded in ordinary
business work, with connected people, documents and events.

## The company

Create a UK wholesaler or distributor with customers, suppliers, products, staff
and departments. Explain what it sells, how it operates, who does what and what a
normal working day looks like. Give the company a clear persona, separate from
the job below. Use fictional business information throughout.

## The job description

The employee handles finance, payroll and customer-service administration.

**Finance and accounts**

- Maintain accurate day-to-day bookkeeping and financial records.
- Process purchase and sales invoices.
- Record and reconcile supplier payments and customer receipts.
- Complete bank reconciliations and manage accounts payable and receivable.
- Follow up outstanding customer balances and reconcile account statements.
- Assist with VAT preparation, expenses, month-end and year-end working papers.
- Prepare basic financial and management reports for the directors.

**Payroll**

- Prepare monthly payroll and check hours, salaries, overtime, holidays and deductions.
- Maintain employee payroll records and process starters and leavers.
- Prepare payslip, P45 and other payroll working documentation.
- Assist with pensions administration and handle employee payroll queries.
- Complete the work accurately and on time.

**Customer service and administration**

- Handle customer calls and emails, and take and process orders.
- Answer questions about products, prices, invoices, payments and deliveries.
- Investigate complaints and maintain customer relationships.
- Liaise with warehouse staff, sales, suppliers and transport companies.
- Maintain customer records, price lists and account information.
- Provide general administrative support to management.

The role needs bookkeeping and payroll experience, strong Excel skills,
experience with accounting software such as Sage or Xero, clear communication,
attention to detail and independent prioritisation. Wholesale, food distribution,
import/export or FMCG experience would be useful.

## What to generate

Create a believable period of work with several connected assignments. Include
routine work as well as exceptions: an invoice that does not match a delivery,
a customer payment that needs allocating, an unexplained bank entry, a payroll
input discrepancy and an order or delivery complaint. Let the same customers,
suppliers and colleagues recur so the company feels coherent.

Show both ways people work: using the company's systems directly, and taking
information into a spreadsheet, reconciling it, then updating the system or
sending the result to a colleague. Include realistic emails, working documents,
source evidence and spreadsheets. Some issues should need a colleague's answer
rather than an invented resolution.

The sandbox can represent accounts and orders, HR and payroll records, shared
files, spreadsheets, email, chat and colleague follow-ups. Choose the tools that
fit this company. Where a responsibility cannot be acted out directly, represent
it honestly through supporting evidence or a working-paper task, following the
shared instructions.

Leave the work for the employee to do. Provide separate private reference answers
and expected evidence so someone else can judge the outcome. Deliver the finished
company pack and a short, plain-language explanation of the company, the work
included and anything the simulation cannot demonstrate. Do not run it or change
the live company.
